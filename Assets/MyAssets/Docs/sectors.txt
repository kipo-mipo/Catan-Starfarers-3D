There are 15 total sector slots on the board, and 4 fixed+revealed starting planet sectors. Each sector slot has a star rating of one or two. There are 16 sector pieces to randomly place in the board slots: 8 planetary (5 one star and 3 two star), 4 trade (2 one star and 2 two star), 4 empty (2 one star and 2 two star). So, there will always be an extra sector piece not used. For 5-6 player expansion, there is a different composition: 10 planetary (6 one star and 4 two star), 5 trade (2 one star and 3 two star), 1 empty (1 star). Each planet sector can be oriented in 3 ways, because the rotation relative to board can be different but the planet position remains relative to the piece. Each sector slot has an orientation up or down relative to the board. The up/down orientation is because at a single node where a tile could be, the 3 lanes can be oriented in a "Y" or a "λ" pattern; where Y has a lane pointed down and λ has a lane pointed up, affecting the surrounding 3 hexagons.

There are 10 total shapes of tokens. Every planet on every piece has a corresponding roll token shape which is simply a pool of roll numbers to randomly choose from.  Planets have triangle, square, and hexagon. Starter planets have a, b, c, and d. One and two star planets have the same shapes but different pools. So 10 pools: 3 one star, 3 two star, and 4 starting planet. Some tokens have two roll numbers, yielding resources on either number rolled. The hexagon pool (one and two star) have tokens with special mothership upgrade requirements in order to settle next to those planets. The special tokens have a "reserve token" sub-pool of numbers to randomly select from once requirements are met.
